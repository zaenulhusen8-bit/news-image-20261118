A newspaper front page that sums up Wednesday 18 November 2026 in one
1024×1024 image, built for the [make.fun](https://make.fun/c/news-image-20261118)
news-image-20261118 competition.

## The five stories

1. **The Gulf — war.** Iran's drones target the US radar at Sheikh Isa air
   base in Bahrain; Bahrain intercepts the attacks, Kuwait reports drones,
   and the US strikes back at a missile launch site on Qeshm Island.
2. **Ukraine — war.** The Crimea bridge's road span falls into the sea after
   a second strike in two days, Kyiv says, after Russia hit its bridges.
3. **Asia — diplomacy.** Trump arrives in Manila for Quad talks ahead of his
   China trip; Xi, Putin and Trump are to meet in Shenzhen as APEC gathers.
4. **Markets.** The Dow falls 849 points in a tech sell-off; the S&P 500 sits
   at its lowest since July ahead of Nvidia's results.
5. **Weather.** Cyclone Akvan strengthens over the Bay of Bengal; India warns
   its coasts as the storm intensifies.

Sources: Reuters, AP, AFP, Al Jazeera — reported 18 November 2026.
Illustrations are AI-generated; all headline text is set with PIL so it
stays legible.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) copies `entry.png`
byte-for-byte to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`. Two runs produce
identical bytes.
